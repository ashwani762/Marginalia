# Marginalia motion philosophy

Built with the motion-design skill (three pillars, personality archetypes, Disney principles, choreography rules).

## The feeling

Relief. It is the moment you put the pen down because you finally trust something else to listen.
Everything on screen should feel like a small exhale after a stressful scramble. Warm, quick and a little cheeky. Never corporate, never cold.

## The micro story

The whole video is one story told in four acts.

| Act | Share | What the viewer sees | Motion mood |
|-----|-------|----------------------|-------------|
| Anticipation | 7% | Paper notebook, frantic scribbling, words flying past, a red MISSED IT stamp | Urgent, shaky, too fast |
| Action | 55% | The phone rises, captions appear, a jot gets tidied, old meetings chime in, a question gets an answer | Bouncy, confident, clear |
| Reaction | 20% | 9 minutes becomes 23 seconds, a lock clicks shut, checkmarks land | Surprise, then trust |
| Resolution | 18% | The wordmark settles and the pen drops for good | Calm, one long exhale |

## Personality

One archetype for the whole piece: **Playful**, with a **Premium** ending.
Playful carries the story. The wordmark and the final pen drop borrow Premium (slow, no overshoot) so the video lands calm. We ease into that shift and never snap.

Brand motion identity, three constants:

1. **Signature easing**: ease-out-back with 10 to 20 percent overshoot, used for about 80 percent of motion.
2. **Duration palette**: quick 180 ms, standard 300 ms, slow 450 ms. Camera moves are the only slow ones, 600 ms.
3. **Entrance pattern**: bounce up from below, with a small squash as it lands.

## The rules we keep

- **Three layers in every scene.** Primary is the one thing the eye follows. Secondary is shadows and chips arriving 50 to 100 ms later. Ambient is drifting blobs and slow floating dots that never ask for attention.
- **One hero per beat.** No more than three things move at the same time. Everything else waits for its stagger.
- **Stagger, never sync.** Words reveal 45 ms apart. Total stagger stays under 500 ms.
- **Entrances beat exits.** Exits run at about 70 percent of the entrance time and use ease-in, so things leave quickly.
- **Nothing travels more than a third of the screen without a keyframe.** Long moves are broken with an arc or a speed change.
- **Never linear** for spatial motion. Linear only for the stopwatch digits and the scribbling pen.
- **Counter-motion.** When the phone rises, the background drifts down at about 20 percent of its speed. When a card lands, its shadow spreads.
- **Always a setup, an action and a settle.** Even a chip pop winds up, pops and settles.

## Moments that have to land

| Moment | What happens | Principles |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| MISSED IT stamp | Slams in at 2.4x scale, squashes, then the notebook shakes | Exaggeration, squash and stretch, follow-through |
| Phone rise | Rises with 3 percent overshoot, shadow grows 50 ms after it lands | Slow in and out, secondary action |
| Camera punch-in | 600 ms ease-in-out zoom onto the jot bar and the memory cards | Staging, attention direction |
| The stopwatch | Counts up to 9:05 slowly, then 0:23 snaps in green with a burst | Timing contrast, surprise, exaggeration |
| Padlock | Shackle drops, body squashes, a click | Anticipation, follow-through |
| Pen drop | Falls, bounces three times with less height each time, rests | Weight, solid drawing, resolution |

## Sound

The silent version is the default because LinkedIn plays on mute. The sound version adds soft effects that match the motion: pops, whooshes, a thud for the stamp, ticks for the stopwatch, a chime for 23 seconds and a click for the lock. No music. Keep it low.

## Accessibility

Nothing important is communicated by motion alone. Every claim is also on screen as text. The photo slides are the reduced motion version of the same story.
